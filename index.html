SSciar Tips For restaurant owners

Weekly card-tip payouts

A tip split set by your restaurant.

Sciar Tips puts card tips into a weekly pool and distributes payouts using the role points and hours in the split rule you set.

The process

One pool. A rule you set. A payout for each person.

Here is how the Sciar Tips arrangement is described in the case pack.

01

Set the split rule

The restaurant owner sets the rule in the owner dashboard.1

02

Share the weekly pool

Card tips go into one pool. Role points apply to hours worked up to 23:00; later hours earn no points. Each payout is the pool multiplied by that person’s points, divided by the team’s total points.1

03

Give each person their figure

Staff get a text each Monday with their own payout. They do not see the rule, the pool, or anyone else’s share.1
